# Daily runner observation review

Current release: expand Class in Capture Wall for overall shape, Early T15→T5, Late T5→T30, and Closing T2→T30 movement. Each window includes spread and traded-volume change when available. Complete/Incomplete/Not comparable describe snapshot coverage and scratching breaks, not predictive confidence. Delayed feed and actual capture timings must remain visible. This is a manual observation workflow; queue/emitter behaviour is unchanged.

Next steps, proposed but not implemented:
1. Add explicit Watch / Pass / Review later decisions per race and runner, with a reason and timestamp. Preserve decisions and revisions; no automatic pruning or betting.
2. Add relative market context: same-snapshot normalized probability change, market rank movement, runner traded-volume share and relative spread. Flag stale last-traded prices and gaps rather than assigning unvalidated confidence scores. Recompute or separate periods around scratchings.
3. Combine a small evidence panel: Base with number/age of contributing starts, recent win, and same-distance/class/going history. Keep unknown fields visible. Use these to explain an observation shortlist, not manufacture a win probability.
4. Freeze a pre-start review record and assess stability, missingness and outcomes prospectively. Keep retrospective imports separate. Compare shortlisted observations with the rest of each race before considering threshold changes.

Do not mix per-runner snapshot times in market comparisons, rank incomplete races as complete, or treat market-implied odds as a model prediction. Initial 3% thresholds remain descriptive defaults; tick-size-aware movement and validated thresholds are later refinements.
